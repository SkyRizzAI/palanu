Connect a PalanuOS device

  • USB: plug the board in and pick its serial port (Chrome or Edge on a computer). The cable is trusted: no pairing.
  • Bluetooth: turn Bluetooth on in the device's Settings, then pick PalanuOS (Chrome or Edge).
  • Wi-Fi / LAN: the device and this computer on the same network; type the device's address (Settings > Wi-Fi shows it). Port 7001.

Wireless links pair once: open the pairing window on the device (Settings > Remote > Pair, or link pair in its terminal) and type the 6-digit code here. Everything runs in this page; nothing goes through a server.

Click the screen and type: arrows, Enter, Esc, Tab and letters go to the device.
