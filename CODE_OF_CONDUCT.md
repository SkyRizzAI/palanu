# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its
standard of behavior. In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, or publishing other people's private information.
- Maintainers may remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may ban the people responsible.

Report violations privately through this repository's private reporting feature (Security > Report a vulnerability) or by
contacting the maintainers. Every report is reviewed and handled with the reporter's privacy in mind.
